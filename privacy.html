WickStep

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: WickStep does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no sign-up and no account. Your learning progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

WickStep is a trading-education app published by Gace Ahmad, trading as AYA Group, in Sydney, Australia ("we", "us"). This policy explains how WickStep handles information on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Information we collect

None. WickStep does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We do not create user profiles and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Information saved on your device

To remember where you are up to, WickStep saves the following only on your device: the market and timeframe you learn on, the lessons you have watched, your test scores, points and day streak, which courses you have unlocked, and whether the teacher's voice is on. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Courses are sold as one-off in-app purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment under their own privacy policies. We never see or receive your card or bank details. The app only receives confirmation from the store that a course has been purchased, so it can unlock it and let you restore purchases on a new device.

The teacher's voice

Lessons are narrated with pre-recorded, AI-generated audio included in the app, or with your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. Neither sends anything you do in the app over the internet.

Permissions

WickStep does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

Children

WickStep is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Education only

WickStep is for education only and is not financial advice. Practice charts are simulated and are not live market data.

Changes to this policy

If the way WickStep handles information changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact
